# fitness-app-questionnaire

### Full User Experience questionnaire 

##### Opening Script:
"First off, thank you for accepting this user-experience questionnaire. I’m going to be asking you a handful of open-ended questions to get an idea of what potential users might want on a phone workout app. Please feel free to answer the questions however you see fit. There is no right or wrong answer. My goal is to simply understand your experiences and perspective. So, let’s get started."
  
##### Questionnaire:
Getting to know person
1. Can you tell me a little about yourself and how you typically use apps on your phone in your day-to-day life?
2. What apps do you find yourself using most often?
3. Can you think of an app you’ve visited recently that’s similar to this? "...Let’s use that as a reference"

Pre-Arrival and Arrival
4. When you visit a workout app, like this, what kinds of information do you usually look for first?
5. After getting this information, what would you do next?
6. What type of functionality would you expect the app to have?

Decision
6. What would help you decide whether to use this app?
7. What would make you hesitate? (optional/alternative to above)
8. If you decided you wanted to start using the app, what would you do next?
9. What else would you want to know?
  
Pain Points
8. What would make you stop using an app like this?  
9. Think of a similar app you’ve used recently-what worked or didn’t work about it?
10. Was anything missing?

General
11. Can you describe something you’ve done on a similar app that felt especially helpful?
12. What made that helpful?
13. What other capabilities would you want this app to have?
14. Put them on a scale of most to least important. (optional)
15. If you were to tell a friend or coworker about the app, what would you say?  
  
After looking at prototype
1. How easy was it to navigate between screens? (1–5 scale)
2. Did the mascot animations make the experience more enjoyable?
3. Was anything confusing or hard to find on first use?  

##### Closing Script:
"That’s all of the main questions I have. Thank you for participating in the questionnaire. Last thing, is there anything about how you use apps like this that I should have asked but didn’t?"

"That concludes this questionnaire. Again, thank you for your time. Enjoy the rest of your day."
